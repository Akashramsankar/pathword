How to play

Drop the marble

A 6-letter word is hidden in the shelves, one letter on each, top to bottom. Tap a tile on the glowing shelf to drop the marble onto it.

KRAQ

The word never uses the same column twice.

Trapdoors

The right tile is a trapdoor. It opens, the letter locks green, and the marble falls to the next shelf.

KRAQ
DVEW

The rest of that column crumbles away, so lower shelves get smaller.

The shelf tips

A wrong tile tips the shelf toward the right one. The marble rolls off, and the tile and everything uphill of it fall with it.

CJMSDT

Tipped right: the letter is somewhere right of M. Each roll-off costs one of your 6 marbles.

Freefall

When a shelf is down to one tile, the marble drops straight through it. Freefalls are free and can chain.
